# FINN decisions and limits

## Decisions and tradeoffs
- Static-first: a Python build creates a compact bundle; React ranks and evaluates alerts in-browser. This makes deployment/offline use simple but data remains a dated snapshot.
- Deterministic scoring combines category, subscription, holding weight, price reaction and volume. It is explainable, not a predictive investment model.
- Clustering uses ticker, day and topic. It reduces duplicate cards but is a heuristic, not proof of one event.
- NSE supplies the Sep–Nov 2026 announcement snapshot. BSE access was blocked. Raw caches, committed seeds and Screener discovery support repeatable demos and fallback.
- Replay masks unavailable future price reactions and defaults to the latest completed price session.
- Results are extracted deterministically, period/unit matched, reconciled and cross-checked. Ambiguity or failed validation produces a filing link, never guessed numbers.
- Quick-question chips scroll to financial modules without model calls. They do not provide open-ended advice.
- Any-ticker research runs through a CLI with browser-like headers, throttling and disk caching. Arbitrary tickers must first be built into the static bundle.

## Primary-source disclosure
The research view uses Screener's company-reported annual, quarterly, balance-sheet, cash-flow and ratio tables. It does not parse annual reports or transcripts for these numeric series.
The Infosys narrative sections—promise vs delivered, guidance, narrative vs numbers, bull/bear—are a curated sample written by Claude from five earnings-call transcripts filed with the SEC, plus Screener figures where marked. They are not generated by the app.
The sample discloses that Q2 FY26 and Q3 FY26 guidance excerpts were used without reading those two calls end to end. Management's operating margin and Screener OPM have different definitions.
Management-change biographies are push-side enrichment from public web sources, also curated by Claude. Each entry retains source links and its supplied confidence label; the app does not independently verify those labels.
The two supplied JSON data files are retained unchanged. Rendering resolves transcript references through their source map.

## Deliberately left out
- LLM layer in the app: cost, credentials and evidence controls are deferred.
- AI transcription of failed results filings: only validated extractions receive numeric cards.
- Live Ask bar: no grounded retrieval pipeline yet; Open company provides a useful deterministic action.
- Automated management enrichment and promise-vs-delivered: current samples demonstrate presentation, not automated reasoning.
- Backend and in-app arbitrary-ticker research: a static snapshot keeps the prototype simple and offline-capable.
- Push delivery: alerts are evaluated locally; no notification transport or subscription server exists.

## Honest limits
- Numeric results cards cover 3 companies: TCS, CDSL and KPRMILL. Download success does not imply extraction success.
- Announcements are NSE-only, 15 September–3 November 2026; price replay ends 2 November 2026.
- Holdings and weights are mock. Market prices are sourced, not invented.
- All 20 research tickers now parse all five tables and About, including held-out CAMS; an individual missing row remains unavailable.
- Curated narrative analysis covers one company: Infosys.
- No live app model calls; external curation is explicitly separate from app automation.
